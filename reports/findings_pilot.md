# Pilot findings (exploratory)

74 attempts from 43 UFC fights (bell-censored attempts left out). Generated by `research/model/findings.py`. **Exploratory:** these are descriptive rates, not the pre-registered test. The sample is finish-enriched (finish-list fights), so the overall rate is far above the real-world one; compare rows with each other. The 95% intervals are wide: overlapping intervals mean no clear difference yet.

## Seconds on the neck

- Finishes: median **9.6 s** (middle half 6.5–12.9 s), n = 40.
- Failed attempts: median **9.6 s** (middle half 6.2–15.4 s), n = 34.
- For comparison, lock to unconsciousness averaged 9.0 s in 81 MMA choke-outs (Stellpflug 2020).

## Where the attempts come from

candidate_round: 10, finish_list: 64

Failure rounds and the random subset are what make these rates honest; they're the next labeling priority.
