74 attempts. Generated by `research/model/hp_check.py`. HP settings come from physiology and judgement; only the top row is the setting in use, the rest show how much that choice matters.

**How to read it:** most finishes end before HP reaches 0, because a fighter usually taps while still conscious, so HP 0 is not a forecast of the tap. Some failures reach HP 0, so the meter can overstate the danger. The lowest HP separates finishes from failures only weakly: AUC 0.62, about 0.49-0.75, a range that touches chance (0.50). It is also partly about time, since longer attempts drain more HP. HP is a readable estimate for viewers, not a validated measure, and the setting stays at the published 9 s.
