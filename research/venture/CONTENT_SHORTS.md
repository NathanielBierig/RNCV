# Content idea: "learn grappling from UFC chokes" Shorts

> Venture session, 2026-10-18. A quick evaluation. RPM and growth figures are **estimates**. This is not legal advice.

**Pitch.** Make 30–60 s vertical clips of a UFC rear-naked choke (RNC) with the live finish-probability bar. A voiceover teaches one beginner lesson per clip ("watch the second hook: that's why the bar jumps"), and each lesson is backed by a finding from `research/rnc_science/FACTORS.md`. This is the FightLabs-style breakdown format with a data twist that no breakdown account seems to have. I couldn't verify FightLabs' own size or method.

## Copyright: the honest version
- **Reposting UFC footage with an overlay = high risk.** Content ID auto-matches broadcast footage. Expect claims that block or demonetize the clip, or send its revenue to UFC. Manual DMCA notices bring **strikes**, and 3 strikes in 90 days ends the channel ([explainer](https://www.daeryunlaw.com/ru/practices/detail/youtube-copyright)). UFC has sent repeated takedowns even against commentary that used only a few stills ([EFF, "Boxing Now"](https://www.eff.org/press/releases/youtube-user-fights-unfair-takedown-campaign-ufc)). Breakdown creators like BJJ Scout have argued with UFC over fair use too ([Sherdog](https://sherdog.com/news/articles/Opinion-Smarter-Ideas-for-the-UFCs-Intellectual-Property-Plans-99773)).
- **Fair use is a defence, not a permission.** Short clips, heavy commentary, a teaching purpose and no market substitute all help. But it's decided case by case, *after* the claim, and a channel can't monetize or grow while it's fighting claims. TikTok and IG have no meaningful counter-notice for this.
- **Safer format (recommended): no UFC pixels.** Render the **skeleton only** (pose keypoints on a dark background) plus the probability bar, a stats chart (e.g. finish rate by hook type with confidence intervals) and your voiceover. Name the fight and timestamp so viewers can go watch it legally. Keypoints derived from footage are still a grey area, but far lower risk than the broadcast pixels. Add **your own gym rolls** (consented) for the "here's how it feels" demo. Bonus: the skeleton look *is* the brand, and it shows off the CV tech.

## Money (estimates)
- **Shorts RPM ≈ $0.05–0.30 per 1,000 views**; sports runs about $0.10–0.30, with a US-heavy upside of ~$0.50 ([ShortSync](https://www.shortsync.app/resources/how-much-do-youtube-shorts-pay-2026), [Satura football model](https://saturaai.com/football-youtube-shorts-rpm)). So 1M views ≈ $100–300. To earn $500/month you'd need roughly 2–5M views a month. TikTok/IG creator payouts are similar or worse for short clips.
- Ad revenue alone won't pay. The real value is the **audience** for: (1) the danger meter pitch ("my overlay gets X views per post" is the strongest email you can send a promotion), (2) tournament-highlight customers (local competitors follow BJJ accounts), (3) an email list for a later gym/app product, (4) employers (a public, measurable reach story).
- Longer-term money: sponsorships (gi/rashguard brands, instructional sellers' affiliate links) once you have ~10k+ engaged followers (est.).

## Comparables
BJJ Scout (long-form breakdowns, has had UFC friction), MMA commentary channels like Boxing Now (takedown history), and the many IG breakdown pages that repost clips (usually unmonetized and fragile). None I found show a **data-backed probability overlay**, so that's the hook.

## 2-week test plan
| Days | Do | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Build a skeleton-only render template from `tools/overlay` (dark background, two coloured skeletons, the bar, a captions slot) | 1 template |
| 4–14 | Post **6 Shorts** to YouTube Shorts + TikTok + IG Reels (same file): 4 skeleton-only UFC RNCs (one lesson each, from the factor table), 2 own-gym rolls | 18 posts |
| Each post | 1 lesson, a hook in the first 2 s ("this bar predicted the tap 4 s early"), a CTA: "which choke next?" | |

**Metrics (per post, at 72 h):** views, average % viewed (target ≥ 70%), follows per 1k views (target ≥ 3), saves/shares, comments asking questions.
**Kill criteria at day 14:** median < 1,000 views per post across platforms *and* < 2 follows per 1k views → stop, and keep the template as a portfolio asset. **Any** copyright claim on a skeleton-only post → stop UFC-derived posts and switch to gym footage only. **Continue** if at least one post reaches 10k views or the follow rate is ≥ 5 per 1k views.
