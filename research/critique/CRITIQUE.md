## Things that are fine (don't change)

- **The current VICOS split design** (build-phase2's 16:53 version): videos mapped to 6 sequences by image-id prefix, cameras kept together, and a 300-frame purge. The camera-to-sequence mapping matches my independent check (label agreement at the same frame number is 0.84–1.0 within a sequence and about 0 across sequences). Keep the purge.
- **The harness architecture:** group-only CV with `assert_no_group_leak`, a persisted lock, fight-level bootstrap, paired bootstrap against the baseline, and a "CI lower bound > 0" rule for `beats_baseline`. All 6 tests pass (20 s). The hysteresis segmenter handles cut resets correctly in my edge tests (a cut exactly at segment start, a mid-segment cut, and the min-length filter).
- **The eligibility rule** "duration ≥ t + 2 s" is the right leak guard. Keep reporting n per horizon.
- **The must-beat stats-only baseline and the "report it if we lose" stance** (PLAN.md:187). This is the most valuable sentence in the plan. Only fix B1 (#7) and the leak in B2 (#8).
- **Successes anchored to ufcstats finish time ±3 s** (LABEL_SCHEMA.md "Clock"). It's a cheap, objective QA check. Use it to estimate the clock-to-video offset for each clip.
- **The candidate list being derived-only** (no scraping, CSV snapshot, licence recorded), and **"replays are never separate attempts"**.
- **The FACTORS.md analysis plan:** pre-registration, Wilson intervals, fighter-clustered SEs and honest power numbers (I re-derived about 88% and about 48% for 25- and 15-point gaps). Keep it, and pre-register *before* the pilot.
- **Labels-only release for D**: still the right legal shape.

## The 3 cheapest experiments that cut the most uncertainty

1. **UFC pose + back-control smoke test with a stopwatch (about 3 h, laptop CPU, no Kaggle).** Take 3 legally viewable RNC clips (about 60 s each) and run RTMO-m on the CPU. Hand-tag back control at 1 fps (about 180 frames). Measure (a) % frames with both fighters' heads and arms plausible, (b) recall of the VICOS classifier vs the geometry rule on those frames, and (c) CPU fps. This settles #1, half of #2 (whether local CPU pose is feasible) and the 2b gate in one sitting.
2. **15-attempt pilot from a random sample of fights, double-coded (about 5 h).** Draw 8 fights at random (seeded) from successes ∪ candidates for 2022+. Label every RNC attempt in every round under **both** start/escape definitions (#4), logging `search_min`/`label_min`. Output: the real time per attempt (#14), the real ratio of success to failure in a fight-based frame (#3), the real "1 in 3 candidates" hit rate, and how much the two definitions disagree, which picks the schema.
3. **Leak-free B2 on stats only (about 2 h, no video).** With `raw_sources/` alone, compute priors as of `date` and predict "fight ended by RNC | a sub attempt was scored in the round" with grouped CV, once with as-of priors and once with full-career priors. The gap measures how much #8 would have inflated B2. The as-of AUC is the bar Phase 5 has to clear, and it's known before any labeling hour is spent.
