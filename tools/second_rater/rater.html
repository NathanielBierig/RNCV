Thanks for helping. This checks whether two people see the same details in UFC rear-naked chokes.

For each attempt: open the link (it starts about 4 seconds early), watch until the video timer reaches the pause time, pause, and answer what you see at that moment. Don't watch ahead to the end of the choke. If you can't see something, choose "can't tell". The attacker is named on each card. Picture guide: ask for the code book.

Definitions: under the chin = forearm on the neck, not on the jaw. Hand hidden = the choking hand is tucked behind the head or shoulder, out of reach. Two-on-one = both of the defender's hands are on the choking arm. Flattened = the defender is flat on his stomach.
