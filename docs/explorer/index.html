How the finish chance works

Each visible detail multiplies the odds of a finish. The model starts from expert estimates and a published-evidence review, then 71 hand-labeled UFC attempts from 42 fights pull the numbers toward what actually happened.

Tested on fights it never saw, it is only slightly better than guessing the average from a single snapshot (Brier 0.245 vs 0.254), and clearly better when it follows the position as it changes (0.224 vs 0.254).

How HP works

HP is a blood-flow dose, not a forecast. Each position squeezes with a strength from 0 to 1: a locked figure-four under the chin about 0.9, one arm about 0.45, over the jaw 0.05. A full squeeze empties HP in 9 seconds, the average from lock to unconsciousness in 81 MMA choke-outs (Stellpflug et al., 2020). In the labeled UFC finishes, the choke was on the neck for a median 9.6 s.
