1 of 12 - choke_arm_position

Where is the choking arm?

Is the arm under the chin, or up over the jaw?

Look at the side view of the head. Arm on the throat = under_chin. Arm sitting on the chin, mouth or cheek = over_jaw.

1under_chin

Arm crosses the throat, below the chin.

2over_jaw

Arm rides up over the chin or jaw, on the face.

3unclear

You cannot tell from this angle.

2 of 12 - forearm_placement

What part of the forearm is on the neck?

Blade, V-elbow, or across the jaw?

Picture a cross-section of the neck, seen from above. Proposed definitions from FACTORS.md, to be refined.

1blade

Bony edge of the forearm cut straight across the throat.

2v_elbow

Elbow centered under the chin, biceps and forearm on the two sides of the neck.

3across_jaw

Forearm lies across the jaw / lower face instead of the throat.

4unclear

Not visible.

3 of 12 - rnc_style_at_L

How are the hands set up?

Which grip is it? (Red arm chokes, blue arm is the second arm.)

Seen from above, attacker behind. Choose the grip that is on at the freeze frame, not the one you think comes later.

1figure_four

Choking hand grips the other biceps; second hand behind the head. The classic mata leao.

2palm_to_palm

Hands clasped palm to palm, forearm across the throat.

3gable

Thumbless clasp (gable / S-grip) behind the neck.

4wrist_pull

Second hand grabs the choking wrist or forearm and pulls it across. Nothing behind the head, no figure-four. The Dagestani style.

5short_choke

Arm only partly across; blade on the throat, second hand pushes the elbow or head.

6one_arm

No second hand on the choke. Other arm only controls the body.

7unclear

Grip hidden by angle, cut or the ref.

4 of 12 - choking_hand_tucked

Where does the choking hand end up?

Is the choking hand tucked away deep, or just gripping?

Definition still to confirm. Tucked = hidden deep, on the far biceps or shoulder.

1True (yes)

Yes: hand buried on the opposite biceps or shoulder.

2False (no)

No: hand just holding on, in view.

5 of 12 - second_hand_on_traps

Where is the second hand?

Is the second hand on the defender's trap / shoulder, instead of behind the head?

Common in short-choke and wrist-pull styles.

1True (yes)

Yes: second hand on the trapezius or shoulder.

2False (no)

No: behind the head, on the choking arm, or not used.

6 of 12 - squeeze_direction

Which way is the squeeze going?

Where is the attacker pulling or turning the defender?

Probably hard to see on broadcast. If you cannot tell, leave it blank.

1straight_back

Pulling straight back into the chest.

2tilt_choke_side

Tilting toward the side of the choking arm.

3tilt_away

Tilting away from the choking arm.

4pull_up

Lifting the chin and head upward.

5unclear

Not visible.

7 of 12 - control

What are the legs doing?

Hooks, body triangle, or neither?

Look at the attacker's legs on the defender's hips and belly.

1two_hooks

Heels hooked inside both thighs.

2body_triangle

Legs locked around the belly, ankles crossed.

3one_hook

Only one leg in.

4none

No leg control (standing, riding with weight, or legs out).

8 of 12 - defender_two_on_one

What are the defender's hands doing?

Are both of the defender's hands on the choking arm?

This is the "two on one" hand fight. One hand, or none, counts as No.

1True (yes)

Yes: both defender hands pull on the choking arm.

2False (no)

No: one hand, or none, on the arm.

9 of 12 - chin_tucked

What is the defender's chin doing?

Is the defender's chin tucked down?

The defender's first line of defense. Judge at the freeze frame.

1True (yes)

Yes: chin down against the chest, throat covered.

2False (no)

No: chin up or level, throat exposed.

10 of 12 - opponent_flattened

Is the defender flat?

Is the defender flat on the mat?

Flat means back or chest on the mat, so he cannot turn in.

1True (yes)

Yes: flat on belly or back, hips down.

2False (no)

No: up on a side, hip, hands or knees.

11 of 12 - back_context

Where are they?

Where does the back attack happen?

The setting of the whole attempt at the freeze frame.

1ground_back

Classic back mount: both on the floor, attacker behind.

2turtle_ride

Defender on hands and knees, attacker riding on top.

3standing_back

Both on their feet, attacker behind with back control.

4scramble

Moving, no stable position yet.

12 of 12 - attacker_side

Which arm is choking?

Is it the attacker's left arm or right arm?

Picture yourself standing behind the defender, looking the same way he is. Left arm = comes up on your left.

1left

Choking arm is the attacker's left.

2right

Choking arm is the attacker's right.
